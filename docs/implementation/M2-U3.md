## Objective

Evaluate supplier-stage requirements deterministically and preserve uncertainty,
negative findings, and genuine evidence conflicts.

## Approved scope and implementation plan

1. Add pure domain evaluators for material, certification, general MOQ versus
   requested quantity, and supported boolean/categorical capabilities. Accept
   validated Brief requirements, requested quantity, supplier catalog facts,
   and supplier evidence. Return the requirement ID where one exists, a status
   of `MEETS`, `DOES_NOT_MEET`, `UNKNOWN`, or `CONFLICT`, and relevant evidence
   IDs in deterministic order. Produce a separate derived MOQ compatibility
   result when the Brief has no explicit MOQ requirement.
2. Use only the fact and negative representations already present in the M2-U1
   domain and fixtures. A catalog claim without matching evidence or support
   does not automatically establish `MEETS`; missing or inconclusive support
   remains `UNKNOWN`. The absence of a requested value from a non-exhaustive
   capability list does not establish `DOES_NOT_MEET`. Explicit negative
   information can establish `DOES_NOT_MEET`. Reserve `CONFLICT` for
   incompatible evidence about the same semantic fact in the same context;
   do not create a conflict from different contexts or mere missing data.
3. Compare a supplier's general MOQ with the requested order quantity. An
   explicit acceptable MOQ requirement may tighten, but not loosen, that
   threshold. Preserve the derived MOQ result separately from persisted Brief
   requirements; its later presentation or persistence belongs to M2-U4.
4. Leave unsupported and quote-dependent requirements informational at this
   stage. Add no numeric scores, generalized expression language, AI calls,
   schema changes, fixture changes, or new dependencies.

## Explicitly out of scope

Candidate or evaluation persistence, API responses, supplier review UI,
shortlisting, staleness, explanations, quote/comparison evaluators, and M2-U4
or later implementation work.

## Acceptance criteria

- Supported supplier-stage requirements return deterministic, explainable
  states and stable relevant evidence IDs.
- Missing evidence or support yields `UNKNOWN`; an unsupported catalog claim
  alone never becomes `MEETS`, and an absent capability is not treated as a
  negative fact.
- Only incompatible evidence for the same claim and context yields `CONFLICT`.
- General MOQ compatibility is evaluated against requested quantity, with an
  explicit acceptable MOQ limit tightening it when present; a derived result
  exists even without a separate MOQ requirement.
- No numeric supplier score is produced, and non-evaluable requirements stay
  informational.

## Tests and evals
